Help

About This App

Responsible Roommates is an app designed to help roommates manage and keep track of shared expenses and chores, making it easier to stay organized and know what needs to be done.

Managing Roommates

In Main, Enter a roommate's name in the text box. Click "Add Roommate" to add them.

Splitting Expenses

In Main, click "+ Split an Expense". Enter the expense name and it's total cost. Select who should split the cost, then click "Add Expense".

Managing Chores

In Chores, click "+ Add Chore". Enter the chore name, due date, and the roommates responsible for the chore. Click "Add Chore" to save it.

When a chore is finished, click the "Complete"

Viewing History

The History page keeps track of past actions, including added roommates, expenses, and chores.

You can delete individual history entries using the "Delete" button.

Clearing All Data

The "Clear All History" button on the History page removes all history, roommates, expenses, and chores.

Your Data

Information like the names of your roommates, the chores you enter and the expenses will be stored untill deletion.
